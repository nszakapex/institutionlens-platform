# InstitutionLens Brand System 1.0

## Brand idea

**Clarity before coverage.**

InstitutionLens is an institutional-fit intelligence system for financial-technology vendors and advisory firms selling to banks and credit unions. It converts public financial evidence, client knowledge and explicit fit logic into a prioritized institution universe with reasons a team can inspect.

The brand should feel like a private analytical instrument: calm enough for an executive meeting, exact enough for a model review and clear enough for a coverage team to act on.

## Positioning

**Category:** Institutional fit intelligence  
**Primary promise:** See which institutions fit your portfolio—and why.  
**Functional value:** Turn a broad market into a defensible account thesis.  
**Emotional value:** Confidence that attention is being placed deliberately.  
**Audience:** Strategy, growth, product and coverage leaders at vendors and advisory firms serving financial institutions.

InstitutionLens is a decision layer—not a contact database, outbound automation platform or opaque lead-scoring algorithm. It strengthens the decision upstream of outreach and can complement a CRM, contact provider and account-planning workflow.

## Brand principles

1. **Exact** — Use specific evidence, timeframes and institutional language.
2. **Discreet** — Treat client knowledge and institutional context with restraint.
3. **Explainable** — Preserve the path from score to factor to evidence.
4. **Institutional** — Favor durable systems over trend-led fintech aesthetics.
5. **Useful** — Every visual and sentence must improve a decision.

## Identity

The optical mark uses four graphite planes and one Signal Blue focal axis. The construction represents a broad field being narrowed into a selected point.

Primary asset: `assets/institutionlens-mark.svg`

Rules:

- Preserve clear space equal to the width of one outer plane.
- Minimum digital mark size: 20px.
- Use Lens Black on Ledger Ivory whenever possible.
- On dark fields, place the mark on an Optic White tile or create an approved reverse asset.
- Do not rotate, outline, compress, shadow, glow or animate the logo.
- Do not use a magnifying-glass icon as an alternate mark.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Ledger Ivory | `#F5F1E9` | Primary environmental surface |
| Optic White | `#FBFAF7` | Raised interface and document field |
| Lens Black | `#111310` | Primary type and structural lines |
| Signal Blue | `#0A52D6` | Evidence, focus and action |
| Ledger Steel | `#64655F` | Secondary copy and reference data |
| Private Night | `#111719` | Security, privacy and deployment sections |

Signal Blue should occupy no more than roughly 8% of a composition. It indicates information that changes fit, the currently selected object or a deliberate action. It is not a background decoration.

## Typography

The system bundles two open-source typefaces locally:

- **Newsreader:** brand statements, section headings, institutional names, account theses and large numeric moments.
- **Manrope:** navigation, controls, body copy, tables, evidence labels, captions and metadata.

Recommended scale:

| Role | Size | Line height | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Display 01 | 88–164px | 0.84–0.90 | Brand-level statements |
| Display 02 | 52–88px | 0.94–1.00 | Section decisions |
| Editorial | 26–42px | 1.08–1.16 | Account theses and reasoning |
| Body | 16–18px | 1.50–1.60 | Explanatory copy |
| Evidence | 10–12px | 1.25–1.40 | Uppercase, tracked metadata |

Avoid default tech-startup typography, excessive bold weight and all-caps headlines. Uppercase belongs only to evidence labels and small navigation signals.

## Layout

- Use large negative space and one dominant anchor per viewport.
- Build on hairline rules, open grids and aligned evidence columns.
- Let editorial type carry the emotional weight; let interface type carry precision.
- Use cards only for actual interactive objects or bounded records.
- Prefer a full composition over a stack of floating panels.
- Corners should be square or nearly square. Avoid pill-shaped UI except for functional status controls.

## Financial SVG language

Reusable assets:

- `assets/financial-field.svg` — peer movement and converging signals.
- `assets/fit-signal.svg` — factor composition and confidence.

Every visual primitive has a defined meaning:

- **Graphite grid:** stable reference field or peer context.
- **Graphite node:** observed institution or contextual datapoint.
- **Blue node:** material evidence that changes fit.
- **Drawn trace:** change through time or an evidence path.
- **Focus rings:** selection, convergence or confidence.
- **Crosshair:** the current decision object.
- **Blue bar:** the factor or account currently in focus.

Do not use random particles, decorative market charts, neon glows, simulated trading terminals or animated numbers without a clear source and meaning.

## Motion

Motion is diagnostic, not theatrical.

- **Reveal:** 600–1,400ms using `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`.
- **Scan:** 2,400–4,000ms using a quiet ease-in-out loop.
- **Ambient:** 10–16 seconds, low amplitude, used only for a hero or system field.
- Provide a complete `prefers-reduced-motion` state.
- Never animate the logo.
- Stop motion from competing with reading or interaction.

## Voice

InstitutionLens sounds like an experienced strategy partner: concise, specific and willing to show the reasoning.

### Use

- “Five institutions moved into focus after deposit pressure increased.”
- “Fit increased because need, timing and relationship access aligned.”
- “Public evidence. Client-owned logic. Auditable reasons.”
- “Start with one portfolio and one commercial question.”

### Avoid

- “Unlock game-changing, AI-powered leads at unprecedented scale.”
- “Our proprietary algorithm says this account is hot.”
- “Supercharge your pipeline.”
- “Revolutionary insights.”

Write short declarative headlines. Follow each important claim with the evidence, mechanism or limitation that makes it credible. Avoid hype, unexplained acronyms and anthropomorphizing the model.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Brand idea:** Clarity before coverage.
2. **Primary promise:** See which institutions fit your portfolio—and why.
3. **Category:** Institutional fit intelligence.
4. **Product explanation:** Public financial evidence and client knowledge become ranked institutions with explainable fit.
5. **Proof:** Approved sources, client-owned logic, private delivery and account-level reasoning.
6. **Engagement:** Prove the lens on one portfolio through a focused six-week pilot.

## Application checklist

Before approving an InstitutionLens page, email, deck or interface, confirm:

- There is one obvious decision or message.
- Signal Blue indicates evidence, focus or action.
- Claims retain a visible reason or source.
- The typography feels editorial and exact, not generic SaaS.
- Financial graphics encode real concepts.
- No fake customer logos, testimonials or performance statistics are used.
- Illustrative institution names and values are labeled.
- Security and client ownership are described without making unsupported compliance claims.
- Mobile preserves the same hierarchy rather than simply stacking every desktop object.
