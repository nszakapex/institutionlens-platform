Questions

What serious teams ask first.

Is this a lead database?

No. InstitutionLens is a fit and prioritization layer. It can work with your existing CRM and contact providers, while focusing on which accounts deserve attention and why.

Can our team change the model?

Yes. The model is designed to be inspected and calibrated with your experts. Weights, exclusions, overrides and supporting evidence remain explicit.

What do we need to start?

A defined product or advisory thesis, access to a small group of subject-matter experts and—optionally—a client or opportunity export for validation.

Does it replace our CRM?

No. It strengthens the decision upstream of the CRM and can produce account-level context for the workflows your team already uses.
